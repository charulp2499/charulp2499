## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **AI Engineer and published AI researcher** with 3+ years of experience developing and deploying intelligent systems across healthcare AI, computer vision, enterprise automation, and generative AI.

My approach combines research-driven problem solving with practical engineering. I enjoy working across the complete AI lifecycle: understanding a problem, designing an approach, curating data, training and fine-tuning models, optimizing inference, and integrating the resulting systems into production environments.

- 🎓 **Education:** M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence, Gold Medalist.
- 🔬 **Research:** Academic research collaborations with IIT Bombay and IIT Hyderabad.
- 📚 **Publications:** 5+ peer-reviewed research publications across biomedical AI, computer vision, and applied machine learning.
- 🏆 **Recognition:** Best Practical Paper Award at AIxIA 2023 for work on thermal imaging-based night-vision face detection.
- 🏥 **Industry:** Production AI development across healthcare intelligence, clinical workflows, speech AI, and enterprise document automation.
- 🌐 **Focus:** Building reliable, scalable, optimized AI systems that solve measurable real-world problems.

> **My engineering philosophy:** A model is only one part of an AI solution. Data quality, evaluation, inference efficiency, reliability, integration, and measurable outcomes matter just as much.
